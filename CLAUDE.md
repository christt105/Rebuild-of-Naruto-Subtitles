# Rebuild of Naruto — Spanish subtitle translation

This repo is an English `.srt` archive for the *Rebuild of Naruto* fan edit,
plus a from-scratch Spanish (Spain) translation of it under
`Spanish Translation/`.

To translate an episode: read `Spanish Translation/AGENTS.md` first — it's
the short, complete operating procedure (what to read, how to batch the
work to stay token-efficient, terminology precedence, staging, how to
finish). `Spanish Translation/WORKFLOW.md` covers the higher-level policy
(branch/PR conventions, CI gate) that `AGENTS.md` assumes.

Don't re-derive the process from scratch or re-read the whole glossary —
both documents, plus the scripts they point to
(`Spanish Translation/scripts/`), already encode it.
