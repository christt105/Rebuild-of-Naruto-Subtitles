# Season 01 - Naruto: arc primer

Reusable context for translating any episode in this season. Written once,
loaded alongside the glossary for every episode task in this arc — not
rebuilt per episode.

## Setting
Hidden Leaf Village (Konohagakure). Academy graduates are split into
three-person genin squads under a jōnin sensei.

## Main characters present this season
- **Naruto Uzumaki** — loud, impulsive, informal speech even with superiors.
- **Sasuke Uchiha** — terse, cold, addresses peers dismissively.
- **Sakura Haruno** — formal/polite with sensei figures, sharp with Naruto.
- **Kakashi Hatake** — team 7 sensei, relaxed/ironic tone, calls team by first name.
- **Iruka Umino** — academy teacher, warm, uses `-kun`/`-chan` with students.
- Other genin squads and their sensei (Ino-Shika-Chō trio, Team Guy) appear
  from the Chūnin Exam arc onward (~S01E06+).

## Register notes
- Genin talk to each other informally (`vosotros`, `tú`), never `usted`.
- Students address sensei/adults with mild formality but not `usted` —
  Spain anime dub convention keeps peer-level informality even toward
  teachers in a ninja-academy setting.
- Honorifics (`-sensei`, `-san`, `-chan`, `-kun`) are kept, per glossary.

## Continuity notes
- S01E01-05: team formation, D-rank missions.
- S01E06 onward: Chūnin Exam arc begins (written exam, Forest of Death,
  preliminaries) — introduces most of the secondary genin cast at once.

## Known ambiguity risks for this arc
- Group scenes with multiple genin squads on screen (exam rooms, Forest of
  Death) — the `.srt` has no speaker labels, so lines with no clear
  addressee/reply should be flagged rather than guessed.
