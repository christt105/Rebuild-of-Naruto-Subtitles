## `{NC}` — Nine-Tails / Nine-Tailed Fox / Demon Fox / Nine-Tailed Demon Fox

Introduced in: S01E01

Not in `naruto_glosario_es_es.yml` despite being arguably the single most
central recurring term in the whole series (Kurama/the Nine-Tails, sealed in
Naruto). The English source uses several interchangeable epithets for the
same being: "Nine-Tails", "Nine-Tailed Fox", "Demon Fox", "Nine-Tailed Demon
Fox". Working choice used in S01E01:

- "Nine-Tails" -> "el Nueve Colas"
- "Nine-Tailed Fox" -> "el Zorro de las Nueve Colas"
- "Demon Fox" -> "el Zorro Demonio"
- "Nine-Tailed Demon Fox" -> "el Zorro Demonio de las Nueve Colas"

Needs confirming against Crunchyroll ES (if a rip exists for an episode
featuring the beast by name) or fixing as project convention, similar to how
Rasengan/Chidori were fixed. Every occurrence in S01E01 is tagged so a future
find-and-replace across the episode is reliable.

## `{SAMA}` — English "Lord X" / "Lady X" honorific forms

Introduced in: S01E01

The English source frequently anglicizes the Japanese honorific "-sama" into
"Lord X" / "Lady X" (e.g. "Lord Third Hokage", "Lord Hokage", "Lady Kushina",
"Lady Hinata", "Lady Hanabi", "Lord Hiashi"). The glossary already has an
(unverified) entry stating "-dono / -sama / -kun / -chan" suffixes are kept
untranslated, by extension of the confirmed "-sensei" rule, but this hadn't
been applied to a case where the English source itself de-suffixed the
honorific into an English title. Working choice for S01E01: render these as
the "-sama" suffix on the name/title instead of translating "Lord/Lady"
literally, e.g. "Lord Third Hokage" -> "Tercer Hokage-sama", "Lady Hinata" ->
"Hinata-sama", "Lord Hokage" -> "Hokage-sama".

This is a first-episode precedent that will recur constantly (any scene with
Hokage, Hyuga clan elders, etc.) — worth confirming deliberately (ideally
against a Crunchyroll ES rip that has one of these exact phrases) before it
propagates through 70+ more episodes. Every occurrence in S01E01 is tagged.

## `{BB}` — "Billboard Brow" (Sakura's forehead nickname)

Introduced in: S01E01

Ino's nickname for Sakura's forehead. A running gag character detail across
the series, not in the glossary. Working choice: "«Frente de Cartel»"
(literal, keeps the "billboard/signboard" image). Alternatives seen in other
Spanish-language Naruto fan material include "Frentona" / "Frente Ancha" —
worth deciding deliberately since it recurs as a character-defining joke.

## `{HF}` — "Head Family" (Hyuga clan)

Introduced in: S01E01

Hyuga clan political structure term (Sōke), not in the glossary. Working
choice: "la Familia Principal". Its counterpart "Branch Family" (Bunke)
doesn't appear yet in S01E01 but will need a matching decision when it does
(e.g. "la Familia Secundaria") — should be resolved together with this one.

## `{DBY}` — "Believe it!" (Naruto's catchphrase / dattebayo)

Introduced in: S01E01

The glossary already has a placeholder entry for "dattebayo" marked
`verified: false` with the note "(sin fijar - ver nota)" — i.e. no working
choice was ever set. The English source localizes it as the exclamation
"Believe it!". Working choice used in S01E01: "¡dalo por hecho!" as a
matching verbal tic/exclamation. This is Naruto's single most iconic line and
will recur in nearly every episode — worth fixing deliberately rather than
letting ad hoc per-episode choices drift.
