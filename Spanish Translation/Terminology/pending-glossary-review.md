# Pending glossary review

Terms tagged inline with `{CODE}` during episode translation, staged here for
a periodic review pass (not resolved per-episode). Once a term is confirmed
against a reliable source (or fixed as this project's own convention), add it
to `naruto_glosario_es_es.yml` and strip the `{CODE}` tag from every episode
that used it.

Append new entries at the end — do not edit existing ones — so concurrent
episode translations don't conflict on this file.

Entry format (parsed by `scripts/build_site_data.py` to show a tooltip for
the tag in the site's episode viewer):

```
### `{CODE}` — short title
Free-text explanation of the term/decision, as long as needed.
```

---

### `{PAWENC}` - Paw Encyclopedia (S01E05)
English "Paw Encyclopedia", the book of cat paw prints Sasuke and Itachi compiled. Translated literally as "Enciclopedia de Patas". The official Spain rendering is unknown.

### `{MATATABI}` - silvervine drink (S01E05)
English "silvervine drink" (the plant cats are drawn to, matatabi in Japanese). Kept as "bebida de matatabi" to preserve the cat-plant reference. Alternatives: "actinidia" or a plain "bebida para gatos".

### `{VENDETTA}` - vendetta (S01E05)
English "vendetta" / "vendetta permit" / "34 vendettas" for the legal revenge-killing arc. Kept as "vendetta" (common in Spain Spanish) with "permiso de vendetta". Alternative: "venganza" / "permiso de venganza".

### `{LOCK}` - Lock Village and Land of Key (S01E05)
English "Hidden Lock Village" in the "Land of Key", later shortened to "Lock Village" and "ninja of the Lock". Rendered "Aldea Oculta del Cerrojo", "País de la Llave", "Aldea del Cerrojo". The official village and country names are unverified (follows the "Aldea Oculta de la Hoja" pattern).

### `{FIRE}` - "Fire Style!" (S01E05)
Bare shout "Fire Style!" (no technique name). Rendered "¡Técnica ígnea!" to match the glossary entry "Técnica ígnea: Gran bola de fuego". Earlier episodes (S01E01-S01E04) render the same prefix as "Elemento Fuego", which conflicts with the glossary; both need reconciling.

### `{PACHINKO}` - Pachinko Jutsu (S01E05)
Combination attack named "Pachinko Jutsu" in the English. Rendered "Jutsu Pachinko". Unknown whether the Spanish edition transliterates or adapts it.

### `{EXPANSION}` - Expansion Jutsu (S01E05)
English "Expansion Jutsu" (Akimichi Multi-Size family). Rendered "Jutsu de Expansión". The glossary has "Multitamaño Parcial" and "Jutsu Super Multi Tamaño" for related techniques, so this may need to align with the "Multi Tamaño" family.

### `{BEETLE}` - Hidden Jutsu: Beetle Sphere (S01E05)
English "Hidden Jutsu: Beetle Sphere" (Aburame technique). Rendered "Jutsu Secreto: Esfera de Escarabajos". Both the "Hidden Jutsu" prefix (secreto vs oculto) and the technique name are unverified.

### `{MIND}` - Mind Transfer Jutsu (S01E05)
English "Mind Transfer Jutsu" (Yamanaka technique). Rendered "Jutsu de Transferencia de Mente". Unverified.
